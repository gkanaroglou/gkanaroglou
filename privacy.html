Privacy and cookies

Last updated 20 October 2026

Who runs this site

This site is run by George Kanaroglou. If you have any questions, email hello@georgekanaroglou.com.

What I collect

Only if you accept cookies, I record:

  • the first page you landed on, the pages you view, when, and for how long
  • how you arrived, such as from LinkedIn, Google or a direct link
  • your approximate location: country, region and city
  • the network you’re connected through, such as your internet provider, or your company or university if it runs its own network
  • your device type, screen width, browser language and time zone
  • clicks on my email address and the “Get in touch” link

I don’t collect your name or email address, and I don’t store your IP address. Network details can sometimes show which organisation you work for. If you decline, none of this is recorded.

How location is worked out

After you accept, your browser asks a service called ipapi.co for your approximate location and network. To answer, ipapi.co sees your IP address. I keep only the country, region, city and network name it returns.

Why I use it

To understand how people find and use this site, so I can improve it. I don’t use it for advertising. I don’t sell or share it, and I don’t combine it with other data.

Consent records

Each time you make a choice, I record the date and time, your choice, what it covered, the version of this policy, a random consent ID and the page you were on. These records don’t include your IP address. I keep them for up to 2 years, even if you clear your data, so I can show that I asked for your consent.

Where it’s stored

The data is kept in a private Google Sheet in my Google account, so Google stores it on my behalf. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic technical logs, including IP addresses, to keep its service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

How long I keep it

I keep visit data for up to 12 months, then delete it. Consent records are kept for up to 2 years.

Your choices

Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page to change your mind at any time.

  • Withdraw consent stops tracking, deletes the cookies from your browser and removes your visit history from my records.
  • Clear all does the same, and also removes your cookie choice and anything else this site stored in your browser. You’ll be asked again next time.

You can also clear cookies in your browser settings. If you have a question or a request, email me.
