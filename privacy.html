Privacy and cookies

Last updated 20 October 2026

Who runs this site

This site is run by George Kanaroglou. If you have any questions, email hello at georgekanaroglou.com.

What I collect

Only if you accept cookies, I record:

  • which pages you visit, when, and for how long
  • the website that sent you here, such as LinkedIn or Google
  • your device type, screen width, browser language and time zone
  • your answers to the questions on the “Work with me” page
  • clicks on my email address and the “Get in touch” link

I don’t collect your name, email address or IP address. If you decline, nothing is recorded.

Why I use it

To understand how people find and use this site, so I can improve it. I don’t use it for advertising. I don’t sell or share it, and I don’t combine it with other data.

Where it’s stored

The data is kept in a private Google Sheet in my Google account, so Google stores it on my behalf. I also get an email alert when someone visits, with the same details but without the random IDs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic technical logs, including IP addresses, to keep its service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

How long I keep it

I keep visit data for up to 12 months. Anything older is deleted automatically every day. I delete email alerts within the same 12 months.

Your choices

You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the homepage. If you decline, or your earlier choice expires, the analytics cookies are deleted from your browser. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings.

The data uses random IDs rather than personal details, so I usually can’t link it to you. If you have a question or a request, email me.
