## Hi there 👋

I'm George. I work in marketing. I'm interested in why people choose what they choose, and how to measure it.

I hold an MSc in Marketing from the London School of Economics and a First Class BSc in International Business from the University of Kent. I was previously Insights Lead for racing driver Sophia Flörsch at Barracudaworks, a sports branding and commercial strategy agency. I work in English and Greek.

## What I do

| Area | In short |
|---|---|
| **Brand strategy** | Positioning, messaging and how a brand compares with competitors |
| **Digital marketing** | Channel strategy, content and paid campaigns measured against real goals |
| **Pricing strategy** | Research into what customers value, then tiers and packages built around it |

All three are built on two foundations: **marketing analytics** and **consumer psychology**.
